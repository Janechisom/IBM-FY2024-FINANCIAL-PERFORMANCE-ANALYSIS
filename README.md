# IBM FY2024 Financial Performance Analysis (Excel Dashboard Project)

I conducted a financial performance analysis of IBM’s FY2024 results using a dataset extracted from SEC 10-K filings. The data was sourced and structured using Claude, then analyzed in Microsoft Excel.

I built a financial performance dashboard focused on:
• Operational efficiency
• Quarterly performance trends
• Segment contribution analysis
• Drivers of Operating Non-GAAP EPS growth

## Core Objective

The central question guiding this analysis was:

 *What is driving the 7.4% growth in IBM’s Operating Non-GAAP EPS?*

---

## Key Analysis Questions

1. What is driving EPS growth?
2. How does revenue trend across quarters?
3. Which quarter contributes most to performance?
4. Are expenses controlled relative to revenue?
5. Were there any unusual cost spikes?
6. Is operating profit stable or improving?
7. Why does Net Income differ from Operating Income?
8. Which segment contributes most to revenue?
9. Which segment underperforms?

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Trends
Revenue showed steady growth across FY2024, peaking in Q4:

* Q1: $14.46B
* Q2: $15.80B
* Q3: $14.97B
* Q4: $17.52B

 Overall: Strong upward trajectory with minor quarterly fluctuation.

---

### Expenses & Efficiency
Operating expenses remained largely stable, with a one-time spike in Q3 due to a pension settlement charge.

 Core cost structure remained well controlled.

---

### Profitability

* Revenue consistently exceeded expenses
* Operating income dipped in Q3 but recovered strongly in Q4
* Net income declined in Q3 due to non-recurring costs, not operational weakness

Core business performance remained stable and resilient.

---

### Segment Contribution

* Software: ~42% of total revenue (primary growth driver)
* Financing & Other: ~3% (lowest contributor)

Software remains the dominant revenue engine.

---

## Key Insight

The 7.4% growth in Operating Non-GAAP EPS is primarily driven by strong underlying operational performance, led by the Software segment and consistent revenue growth.

Although Q3 was impacted by a one-time pension expense, the strong rebound in Q4 confirms operational resilience and efficiency.

---

## Recommendations

• Continue prioritizing investment in the Software segment
• Strengthen cost monitoring to manage non-recurring expenses
• Maintain operational efficiency to support quarterly stability
• Separate one-time costs from core performance in reporting
• Improve underperforming segments for balanced growth
• Replicate successful strategies from high-performing quarters (Q4)
